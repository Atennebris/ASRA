{{ section_label("What subagents do") }}

A subagent is a pre-configured worker with its own name, allowed tools, instructions, and (optionally) its own LLM provider. While a scan is running, the main agent can hand off a bounded, self-contained sub-task to an enabled subagent and keep working on something more important itself — the subagent reports its own result back automatically once it's done. Only enabled subagents are ever visible to the main agent; disabled ones sit here configured but unused.
